## Idea
Deepfakes and manipulated media pose a growing challenge to digital trust, contributing to the spread of misinformation, defamation, and public confusion, particularly across social media and messaging platforms in India. Our proposed solution is a web-based tool that allows users to upload an image or video and receive an instant assessment of its authenticity, classified as either genuine or AI-generated/manipulated, along with a corresponding confidence score. The system leverages an established open-source deepfake detection model to analyze visual inconsistencies and artifacts not readily perceptible to the human eye. By providing an accessible, no-code verification mechanism for citizens, journalists, and platforms, this solution directly supports Digital India’s broader objective of fostering a secure and trustworthy digital ecosystem.
